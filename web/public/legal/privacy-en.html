General Information

Justus Liebig University Giessen (JLU) attaches the greatest importance and significance to the security of your data. Therefore, we inform you as users of the JLU RAG service about the nature, scope, and purpose of the collection and use of personal data and assure compliance with all relevant legal provisions, in particular the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Hessian Data Protection and Freedom of Information Act (HDSIG).

The JLU RAG service, hereinafter referred to as the "Service", provides functions in the field of artificial intelligence (AI). Users may use the Service for knowledge-based research, analysis and answering of queries, for creating content, and as a possible knowledge base for AI agents. This is done on the basis of documents and content made available in the system or via interfaces by users or administrators. To this end, users make inputs (so-called "prompts", and additionally search queries), upload files or select specific knowledge from connected source systems, which are processed by the Service in a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) process in order to generate an output (e.g. text, knowledge graph, audio).

This privacy policy applies to data collected in the context of using the Service. Which data you may personally introduce into the Service (user inputs, so-called "prompts", or uploads) is governed by the Terms of Use for the JLU RAG service. The Terms of Use can be viewed on this website.

Recipient of the Data and Purposes of Data Processing

The recipient of the data transferred in the context of using the Service is JLU (University Computer Center, HRZ). For parts of the received data (transmission of search queries when the optional web search function is activated), processing on behalf of the controller is carried out by Google. JLU processes personal data and transmits it to the named providers only insofar as this is necessary to provide a functioning Service.

Scope and Types of Processed Data

In the operation of the Service, a distinction is made below between personal data required for the performance of the Service (section a) and data that users introduce for processing in the context of using the Service (section b).

a) Required for the Performance of the Service and Processed Internally by JLU

Access Data/Server Log Files

For technical reasons, we automatically collect and store information in so-called server log files that your browser automatically transmits to us. This data includes:

  • IP address of the requesting computer
  • Date and time of the request
  • Access method/function requested by the requesting computer
  • Data transmitted by the requesting computer
  • Access status of the web server (file transferred, file not found, command not executed, etc.)
  • Name of the requested file

This personal data is processed exclusively to provide a technically functional website and to ensure the security of our information technology systems.

Access Data/Login

The access data you enter (JLU account or HRZ user ID) serves only to verify whether you are authorised for access. Your data is not stored but forwarded directly to identity management, which performs the check.

User Profile and Knowledge Bases

When using the application, personal user profiles are created in order to store individual settings as well as user chat histories and self-created knowledge bases on the server and make them available across devices. These profiles serve the convenient use of the application and the provision of content to the respective user. Knowledge bases are deleted automatically when the JLU account of their owner is deactivated. No further evaluation of individual user behaviour takes place. Statistical analyses are carried out only in anonymised or aggregated form in order to evaluate and improve the use of the offering as a whole.

Session Cookies

Our website uses so-called session cookies. Session cookies are small units of information that a web server stores in the working memory of the visitor's computer. A randomly generated unique identification number, a so-called session ID, is stored in a session cookie. These are necessary so that your browser is recognised again after a page change. The session lifetime is tied to the lifetime of the single sign-on (SSO) login. They are automatically deleted when you log out of the application or the browser is closed.

b) Data Introduced by Users in the Context of Using the Service

All content entered by users ("prompts" and "uploads") as well as the resulting knowledge bases are forwarded via a local LLM proxy server to language models (LLMs) that are operated locally on JLU servers.

The connected language models are not trained with your inputs.

If you activate the "web search" (internet research) function, personal data contained in your search queries may be transmitted to external search engine providers (e.g. Google). A data processing agreement is in place with these providers.

Your Rights

Articles 15 et seq. of the GDPR list your rights. You have the right to receive, upon request and free of charge, information about which personal data has been stored about you, the right to rectification of incorrect data, and the right to restriction of processing or erasure of your personal data.

Should you believe that your data has been processed unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. We recommend that you contact the official Data Protection Officer (contact details see above) before lodging a complaint.
