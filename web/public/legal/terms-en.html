1. Scope

Access to the JLU RAG service is tied to the qualifying status of being a member or associate of Justus Liebig University Giessen – students, teaching staff and employees.

2. Services

The service provides artificial intelligence (AI) functions based on a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) approach. Users may use the service for knowledge-based research, analysis and answering of queries, for creating content, and as a possible knowledge base for AI agents. This is done on the basis of documents and content made available in the system or via interfaces by users or administrators. When building their own knowledge bases, users freely decide which knowledge sources they wish to use: individual file uploads, web searches or other connected JLU source systems that are provided centrally. The system allows the creation of knowledge bases and the generation of various outputs (e.g. text, knowledge graph, audio) that are available for further official use and for integration into AI agent systems used for official purposes.

3. Purpose of Use

The service may be used exclusively for the purposes of study, teaching, research, qualification procedures such as doctorates and habilitations, university administration and official duties for which JLU is the responsible body. Users are not entitled to use the service for private purposes. Users may not make their access to the service available to third parties. In particular, they are prohibited from passing on their user ID and password. They are, however, entitled to share knowledge bases with other JLU members or associates within the system and to transmit links to and copies of inputs and outputs of the service to third parties, provided that such transmission is lawful and permissible.

4. General Provisions

Users are responsible for their input (e.g. chat prompts, document uploads to knowledge bases, their own content in connected source systems) and for the further use of the resulting output. Use of the service may produce inaccurate, erroneous or discriminatory output that may contradict actual facts or misrepresent cited sources (so-called hallucinations).

Users must review the output critically, in particular for accuracy and freedom from discrimination. These Terms of Use supplement the general provisions of the connected systems and do not claim to be exhaustive. All users are responsible for complying with these provisions.

5. Obligations of the User

5.1 Rights of Third Parties

The service must be used in compliance with all rights of third parties. Violations may lead to exclusion from the service, without prejudice to any other criminal or civil consequences.

  • Retrieving, offering, uploading or distributing unlawful content, in particular content that violates criminal law, personal rights, licensing law or copyright, is prohibited.
  • Pursuant to Art. 22 (1) GDPR, no automated decisions may be made that produce legal effects concerning the data subject or similarly significantly affect them. The final decision-making authority, in particular for employment and personnel decisions, must rest with a natural person.
  • When transferring data, applicable contractual agreements, statutory provisions and JLU-internal guidelines must be observed (in particular confidentiality and official secrets). Non-public data may be processed in accordance with the information classification.
  • When using the internet search, personal and non-public data from the search queries (separate field in the system) may be transmitted to external search engine providers. Users are themselves responsible for complying with data protection and information security regulations when formulating such search queries. Chat inputs are not forwarded to search engine providers.

5.2 Personal Data of Third Parties

  • When using the local AI infrastructure (local models/local knowledge bases): Personal data (except special categories of data, see below) may be used as input for official purposes, provided it is necessary for the purposes stated above.
  • When using the web search function: Entering personal data into search queries (separate field in the system) must be examined critically. Entering personal data within the meaning of Art. 4 (1) GDPR is to be avoided as a matter of principle, unless it is strictly necessary and lawful for the specific official research.
  • Prohibition of special categories: Entering special categories of personal data pursuant to Art. 9 (1) GDPR is not permitted. These include, among others: data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic data, biometric data, health data and data concerning sex life or sexual orientation.

6. Sanctions for Violations

JLU is entitled to immediately block access to the service if there is reasonable suspicion that the stored data is unlawful and/or infringes the rights of third parties. Reasonable suspicion of unlawfulness and/or infringement exists in particular when courts, authorities and/or other third parties notify JLU thereof. Other violations of these Terms of Use, in particular of the obligations under Section 5 (1)–(2), as well as misuse for purposes other than those stated in Section 3, also entitle JLU to block the user. JLU must inform affected users of the block and the reason for it without delay. The block must be lifted as soon as the suspicion has been dispelled.

7. Data Protection

Information on the handling of personal data in the operation of the service can be found in the separate privacy policy for the "JLU RAG" service.
